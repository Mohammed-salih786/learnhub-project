## 5) Course or design system page principles

- Keep content dense but readable on mobile
- Prioritize quick actions and clear trust signals
- Use strong contrast with subtle elevations
- Reduce decision fatigue in the first-time learner journey
- Maintain modern, trustworthy product styling without generic college-site visuals
